# Changelog

## 1.2.2
- Machines face whoever places them. `/beverages face` turns one in place.

## 1.2.1
- Placed machines show their resource pack model, and a machine's name floats above it while you look at it.
- Settings: click a drink or the default amount and type the price in chat. The settings menu shows drink names.
- New machines charge Money, with a suggested price for each drink.

## 1.0.0
- First Paper version of the Bedrock Beverages add-on: eight brewed and crafted drinks with Strong and Extended versions, the Mug, the Soda Vending Machine and the Beverage Fountain (prices in an item, Vault Money or free).
- Items have custom model data for the optional server resource pack.
